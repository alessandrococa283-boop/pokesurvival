About PokéSurvival

PokéSurvival is a free, unofficial fan project derived from and inspired by PokéSurvivor. It has no purchases, ads, donations or pay-to-win features.

Derived code is distributed under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Resource and sprite credits and licenses are listed in the repository.

Pokémon and related names and characters belong to their respective owners. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
