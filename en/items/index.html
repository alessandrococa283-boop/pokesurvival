PokéSurvival held items

During a run you can find held items in chests and by defeating bosses. You can only hold one at a time: if you find another, you choose which one to keep. They last for the whole run.

  • LeftoversYou recover 1.5% of your HP per second.
  • Choice Band+40% damage, but you can't switch moves.
  • Quick Claw-20% cooldown on all your moves.
  • Focus SashA hit that would knock you out leaves you at 1 HP (every 60 s).
  • Shell BellYou heal from the damage you deal.
  • Amulet Coin+50% PokéCoins at the end of the run.
  • Lucky Egg+30% experience.
  • Life Orb+30% damage, but each attack costs a little HP.
  • Choice Scarf+35% speed.
  • Rocky HelmetFoes that hit you in melee take damage.
  • Assault Vest+20% damage reduction.
